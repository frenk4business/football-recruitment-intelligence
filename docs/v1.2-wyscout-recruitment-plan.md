# v1.2 Wyscout recruitment — registered evaluation plan

Registered before richer feature extraction or viewing v1.2 retrieval results, 2026-10-02. Baseline `15f84263a447b0eb5f77d927693abca1b03eb5d6`. The machine-readable decisions are in `config/v12-expansion.json`. This is a new descriptive retrieval model; frozen `recruitment-fit-v1`, Player DNA, common-profile and translation versions are not changed.

## Population and minutes

Use all five domestic members of the pinned Figshare event/match archives. Derive actual clubs and dates from rosters. Preserve provider IDs and broad GKP/DEF/MID/FWD metadata roles. GK is searchable but has no outfield recruitment model. Never infer CB/DM/W from action locations. Every included appearance must have reconciled nominal regulation minutes from starting XI, substitutions and timed red cards. The existing audited parser is reused without changing its semantics; any unresolved appearance withholds that player-season. Extra time is explicitly unsupported, not truncated or counted as 90 minutes. Record method and quality reasons for exclusions.

## Feature semantics fixed before evaluation

Audit 24 provider-native features: non-penalty shots, box shots, on-target shots; open-play pass attempts, completed passes, completion percentage, forward passes, completed progressive passes, completed final-third entries, completed penalty-area entries, long passes; crosses, completed crosses, key passes; attacking duels, won attacking duels, defensive duels, won defensive duels, aerial duels, won aerial duels; interception-tagged actions, sliding-tackle-tagged actions, clearances and accelerations. All counts become per 90 reliable nominal regulation minutes; completion is completed/attempts ×100 and unavailable with zero attempts.

Use official event/subevent/tag dictionaries, not StatsBomb field names. Pass means eventId 8 (restart passes excluded). Shots mean eventId 10 or direct free-kick shot 3/33; penalty 3/35 excluded. On-target is an explicit in-goal destination tag 1201–1209 or goal 101, with blocked 2101 excluded. It is a tagged-shot measure, not xG. Head/body tag 403 cannot establish headers: omit header counts. Completed is 1801 with no 1802; contradictory/missing outcome makes affected success features unavailable. Duels won use 703 with no 701/702; missing/contradictory outcome withholds success feature. Unknown geometry withholds the affected geometry-derived feature, never zero-fills it.

Coordinates use Wyscout's attacking direction and the existing 105×68 m normalization. Forward means end_x > start_x. Long means Euclidean distance ≥30 m. Progressive completion reduces Euclidean distance to goal centre by ≥30 m with both endpoints in own half, ≥15 m crossing halfway, or ≥10 m with both endpoints in attacking half; backwards-half transitions do not qualify. Final-third entry crosses x=70 m. Box entry ends in x≥88.5, 13.84≤y≤54.16 from outside that rectangle. Interception/sliding-tackle tags are reported as provider-tagged actions; they are not relabelled as recoveries or StatsBomb pressures. Ground attacking duels are not unqualified dribbles. Accelerations are event annotations, not tracking-derived speed or distance. Assists, recoveries, shot-assist reconstruction and carrying-distance proxies are omitted because a defensible common event definition is absent or unnecessary here.

## Retrieval, chance baselines and uncertainty

For each league and outfield role independently, order each player's appearances by date and match ID and allocate alternating appearances to halves A/B. Full-season thresholds are 450, 600, 900 and 1,200; both halves require at least half that threshold, reliable minutes and all included feature values. Same-role identity retrieval from A to B has one true counterpart. No transfer-success, future performance or target-club-fit labels are invented.

Fit feature scales on A only, within the same provider/competition/season/role. Use equal family weights, equal feature weights inside each family and Euclidean distance of scaled rates. Constant training features carry no information. Report Recall@5, Recall@10, MRR and cohort sizes per league AND role, plus exact uniform-random Recall@k=min(k,n)/n and MRR=H_n/n. Ties are handled explicitly rather than resolved in favour of matching IDs. Bootstrap paired-query MRR 1,000 times with seed 1202026; report 95% intervals. Report feature-wise A/B Spearman correlations and their median, omitting constant features with an explicit count.

Registered role gate: at least 20 paired players; median feature Spearman ≥0.35; Recall@5 and MRR each ≥1.5× their random expectation. Require complete domestic match/event coverage and reliable participation. Select the lowest registered threshold at which at least two outfield roles pass in that league; other roles remain disabled. If no threshold passes, that league remains searchable only. Do not lower thresholds/floors after results. These are descriptive stability/coverage gates, not externally validated predictive guarantees.

Report sensitivity across all four minute thresholds and top-10 retrieval overlap when each family weight is halved/doubled. Do not select features or weights to maximize reported retrieval. League summaries retain failed roles and the full threshold grid. Club-role context requires at least two eligible roster members; insufficient local context disables that target role.

## Product interpretation

Same-league defaults use native values and league-role distributions. Wider Wyscout discovery compares observed raw rates using the target league-role scale, never a translated value or pooled league percentile. Candidate own-league percentiles are a separate descriptive view, with their cohort identified. No WSL translation model, inferred league strength, cross-provider ranking, current transfer availability or new person identity merge. Old WSL shared URLs remain routed to their original model.

## StatsBomb and metadata

Audit every catalogue scope and exact event/lineup presence before selecting new sources. Complete domestic coverage requires the full expected schedule and unique balanced home/away pairs; near-complete ≥95%, partial ≥25%, sample <25%. Without a defensible denominator report sample/scope limitations rather than claiming completeness. European/tournament observations stay separate from domestic player-seasons. New StatsBomb profiles are searchable and descriptively comparable; no new recruitment scope is enabled without a separate provider-native recruitment evaluation. OpenFootball and SkillCorner do not inherit event-performance capabilities.

## Release checks

Keep all frozen outputs byte-identical. Fixtures cover individual event mappings, missing outcomes/geometry, minutes/cards/extra time, cohort boundaries, stable IDs and source conflicts. Publish derived data only through an additive checksummed allowlist. Validate old routes/URLs, EN/NL, mobile, accessibility, exact counts, <100 ms local filter interactions, lazy detail/recruitment requests, storage and licensed-source provenance. Record failures rather than silently removing hard examples.
