## Catalogue and initial selection

The current StatsBomb catalogue has 80 competition-seasons and 3,961 distinct listed matches. The machine-readable catalogue records each competition/season, gender, match/team counts, dates, match IDs and file hashes. A double-round-robin count is a screening signal; it is not by itself proof of a complete competition. The full-catalogue lineup/file audit will distinguish position/identity availability from event/minute validation, which is performed for the ingested selection.

The initial provider-native expansion selects Premier League, La Liga and Serie A 2015/16 (380 listed matches each), FA Women's Super League 2020/21 (131, explicitly partial) and 2023/24 (132), and Frauen Bundesliga 2023/24 (132). Selection is based on broad match coverage, available positional evidence, both genders, multiple seasons and three men's leagues also represented by Wyscout. The seasons differ between providers; this is a deliberate confound to report, not a paired measurement study. No outcome/model-performance selection is used.

Figshare collection v5 contains seven competitions. The five domestic targets are England, Spain, France and Italy 2017/18 (380 each) and Germany 2017/18 (306): 1,826 matches. The commonly quoted 1,941 includes the 2016 European Championship and 2018 World Cup, which are outside the domestic profile selection. Reported approximately 3.25 million events and 4,299 metadata players concern the entire release; measured domestic actor/profile counts will be reported separately after ingestion.

The `files` records in the audit include exact article URLs, licences and upstream checksums. Downloaded content will additionally be pinned with SHA256. Player metadata actually contains `wyId`, first/middle/last/short name, birth date/area, passport area, current club/national-team IDs, broad role, preferred foot, height and weight. Historical profile club membership comes from match participation, not the metadata's un-timestamped current club. Names remain provider-scoped. Birth/passport data are not used for ranking or eligibility.

## Ingestion and publication decision

Proceed with an additive provider-native adapter and conservative common features. Cache source files in ignored `data/raw/v11/`; partition canonical events and player-match observations in ignored Parquet storage. Only derived searchable summaries/features, source manifests, quality statistics and evaluation results are candidates for publication. Full event archives and copied metadata tables will not be committed or served by Render.

Minutes must come from lineup/substitution/card participation. Match-duration and position uncertainty remain explicit. Searchable profiles, common-feature availability, within-cohort similarity and existing translation eligibility are separate capabilities. Common rates use explicitly reconciled nominal regulation minutes for both providers; they do not silently reuse the elapsed-minute denominator of frozen Player DNA v1. Extra-time games are unsupported by this v1.1 domestic build and must be withheld explicitly.

Final measured counts, exclusions, observed dates, threshold coverage, source sizes and catalogue checks will be appended after the build; these initial quoted dataset totals are not product claims.
