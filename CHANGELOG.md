## 1.0.0

### Data foundation

Pinned source revisions, typed canonical contracts, explicit coordinate/missingness rules, provenance and a bilingual aggregate explorer. Raw restricted provider feeds remain local.

### Player DNA

Eighteen role-aware observed style features, transparent nearest-neighbor comparisons, evidence thresholds, explanations and exploratory PCA. WSL 2023/24 only; one season does not establish future performance or general transfer value.

### Historical translation

A narrower historical WSL study with fixed temporal splits, source/ridge baselines, Bayesian research comparisons, diagnostics and predictive intervals. Broad cross-league translation is NO-GO. No Bayesian candidate qualified as the public default across validation gates; interval undercoverage and confounding remain explicit.

### Recruitment intelligence

Explicit user requirements, observed replacements/club context, directional RMS percentile mismatch, strict hard constraints, comparisons, share links and separate weight/profile sensitivity. Final peer MRR .258 is essentially DNA .259; final roster MRR .232 trails DNA .332. Context evidence is mixed; small candidate pools inflate stability. No transfer-success, availability, fees or club recommendation claim.

### Production hardening

Versioned deterministic release metadata, schema/hash publication gates, runtime integrity/retry boundaries, real Render security/cache headers, protected checked-main deployment, Python 3.12/3.13 and three-browser CI, dependency governance, accessibility/responsive fixes, SEO/404/no-JavaScript handling, reproducibility evidence and operations/rollback documentation. All seven scientific versions and frozen research results remain unchanged. No extra hosted infrastructure or Frenkkester.com changes.
